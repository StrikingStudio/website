Striking Coach.

Share your passion for boxing, kickboxing, or Muay Thai – on your schedule.

Share what you love. Coach on your schedule.

You've put in the rounds. You know what good striking feels like. You move well, you understand the technique, and you bring energy to every session you're part of. Now you can share that – without it taking over your week.

Striking Studio is looking for part-time coaches to lead our boutique heavy bag fitness classes. The role is built for people who train seriously, want to stay close to the sport, and like the idea of earning on the side while doing something they actually enjoy.

You don't need to be a pro fighter. You don't need years of coaching experience. If you train consistently, move well, and want to learn how to lead a class – we'll teach you the rest.

Three reasons to join

  • Live your passion: Coach 2–5 classes a week around your main commitments. Stay sharp, stay connected, and keep building your skills as a striker – by teaching others.
  • Flexible side income: Fair per-class compensation, paid monthly. No long-term commitment. Pick up more classes when it fits, scale back when life gets busy.
  • Grow as a coach: A clear class structure, defined training methodology, and a team that supports you. You'll get better at leading a room and communicating technique.

Your role

  • Coach 55-minute group classes following the Striking Studio format
  • Welcome members and create a high-energy atmosphere
  • Guide first-timers through bag setup, technique, and class flow
  • Maintain studio space and equipment between classes
  • Show up on time, prepared, and ready to lead

Disciplines: Fitness Boxing, Fitness Kickboxing, Fitness Muay Thai. You'll be assigned classes based on your background – you don't need to coach all three.

Who we're looking for

  • A basic training background in boxing, kickboxing, and/or Muay Thai
  • Clean, confident technique on the heavy bag
  • Energy and enjoyment working with people
  • Reliability – punctual, prepared, consistent
  • A fit, healthy, and inspiring presence

Not required: Pro or amateur fight experience, coaching certifications, experience in all three disciplines, years of teaching experience.

Eligibility

  • Location: Based in or near Zürich.
  • Work permit: Swiss / EU-EFTA or valid B/C/L permit.
  • Languages: English required. German is a strong plus.
  • Class times: 07:00 · 12:00 · 17:40 · 18:50 · Saturday 09:40

  • Any coaching, teaching, or instructing experience (if any)
