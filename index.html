FAQ

What’s really holding you back.

The five questions new members ask us most often – usually only after their first class, because they didn’t dare ask before.

I’m not fit enough. Is that a problem?

You are. Every exercise can be adapted and you set your own pace. Most people here started exactly where you are.

Do I have to fight anyone?

No. The bag doesn’t hit back – promise. No contact, no sparring.

Do I need my own equipment?

You don’t. Gloves and wraps are ready for you – sportswear and water are all you need.

Will I be the worst one in the room?

Nobody here is watching how good you are. Everyone has their own bag – and the coach is there for you, not as a jury.

What happens after the trial class?

Nothing automatic. Want more? First month −50%. Not for you? Then it was a free workout – all good.
